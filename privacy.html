Privacy policy for GroceryList

Effective October 23, 2026

GroceryList is an Android app made by DVGApps. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: I do not collect, receive or store your data. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own. A few optional features contact outside services, and those are listed below so you can decide whether to use them.

Who is responsible

DVGApps (Diedrich Guenther), an independent developer. Questions about this policy: dvgapps.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter, including items, prices, photos, stores, aisle layouts, lists, sets and settings, is stored on your phone in the app's private storage. I never receive it. I run no server for this app.

Outside services the app contacts, only when you use a feature

These requests happen only when you use the feature named. They go straight from your phone to the service, not through me. Like any internet request, the service can see your phone's IP address.

FeatureServiceWhat is sent
Scanning or typing a barcode in the item editorOpen Food FactsThe barcode number only. The app then downloads the product's name, brand, size and photo, if the database has them, and saves them on your phone.
Searching for an address on a store's location screenOpenStreetMap NominatimThe address text you typed, and your phone's language setting.
Opening a store's mapOpenFreeMapRequests for the map tiles, fonts and style for the area you are viewing. The app adds no identifier of its own.
Scanning a barcode with the cameraGoogle Play services (ML Kit code scanner)The scanner screen is provided by Google Play services, which processes the camera image. The app receives only the scanned number. Google may collect its own technical diagnostics under Google's privacy policy.

I do not control these services and cannot say what they do with the requests they receive. Follow the links for their policies. If you never scan a barcode, search for an address or open a map, the app makes none of these requests.

Location

Location is optional and off unless you turn on “Auto-select nearby store” or tap “use my current location” for a store. The app reads your position through Android, compares it with the store positions saved on your phone, and does not send it anywhere. The coordinates of stores you save are kept on your phone only. Your phone's own location services are part of Android and are governed by Google's and your device maker's terms.

Camera and photos

To add a photo to an item, the app opens your phone's camera app or photo picker. The photo you choose is copied into the app's private storage. The app does not request camera or storage permission.

Backups

Backups you make

“Back up now” saves a file to a location that you choose. “Restore from backup” reads a file you choose. The app never uploads a backup anywhere on its own. Where you keep the file is up to you.

Android’s automatic backup

If Google’s backup is switched on in your phone’s settings, Android may copy the app’s data, including photos, to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. That is done by the operating system, not by me, and I do not receive it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

What I do not do

  • I do not collect personal information, and I have no accounts or sign-in.
  • I do not show ads and do not use advertising IDs.
  • I do not use analytics or crash-reporting services in the app.
  • I do not sell or share your data. I do not have it.

Children

GroceryList is a general-purpose app and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because I hold none of your data, there is nothing for me to access, correct or delete. You control your data on your phone: you can remove items inside the app, use “Reset all data to stock” in Settings, or uninstall the app to erase its stored data. Backup files you saved and any copy made by Android’s backup are yours to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, for example when purchases are added, I will update this page and its date before the change reaches you.

Contact

Questions about privacy: dvgapps.support@gmail.com.
