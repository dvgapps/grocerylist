GroceryList

GroceryList is a grocery list app for Android that follows the way you actually walk through a store. Each store gets its own aisle layout, and your list is sorted to match, so you shop one aisle at a time.

  • Items with prices, sizes, flavors and photos
  • A separate aisle and department layout for every store
  • Pantry, shopping list, cart and running total
  • Saved sets of items, such as a weekly staples list
  • Your data stays on your phone (plus your own Google backup, if you have that switched on), with no account and no ads
